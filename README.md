# Multi-Missile
Multi-Missile, also known as Multi-Missile Addon Pack, is my personal addition of several missiles, real and fictional, to Nuclear Option. Missile statistics are highly accurate, but still balanced enough to be playable.
Multi-Missile is not currently publicly released. The mod is still in the works, but expect a release in the coming few days.

## Encyclopedia/InGame Definitions:

StormX Shadow:
The StormX Shadow is an advanced redesigned version of the older subsonic Storm Shadow cruise missile. It travels just past supersonic speeds and can be launched by many assault aircraft against ships and ground targets. It is extremely effective for armored targets and in large enough numbers can destroy ship formations. It is the longest-operating-range missile cruise available.

K-30M:
The Vympel K-30, sometimes referred to as the Izdeliye 300M or K-MD, is a Russian IR air-to-air missile that was originally developed during the Soviet Union but was later revived by Russia as the Izdeliye 300M, and was again re-named back to the K-30M when the missile was brought into service. It was created as a direct competitor to the American AIM-9X Sidewinder and supposedly doubles the performance of their legacy R-73 missile, also known as the AA-11 Archer by NATO. AM39E Exocet:
The AM39E (under the same umbrella as the Exocet Block 3C) is a long-range air-launched anti-ship missile, a modernized variant of the older AM39 Exocet line. Unlike older variations, this missile features a booster that helps the missile advance to its top speed of (fill this).

ATMACA-AL:
ATMACA-AL was Turkey's attempt at an air-launched variant of their subsonic ground-launched ATMACA missile after SOM-J. After months of design work and testing, the missile boasted a fantastic sink-success rate on ships that did not fire and is a preferred missile for finishing off damaged or crippled ships. 3M22 Zircon:
The 3M22 Zircon (also known as Tsirkon) is a long-range nuclear-capable hypersonic missile that travels at speeds nearing mach 9. This particular variant carries a (fill this)kg warhead. It's important to be cautious of aircraft weight when carrying the Zircon as it is a heavy and expensive missile.

3N22 Zircon (250kt):
The 3N22 Zircon (also known as Tsirkon) is a long-range nuclear hypersonic missile that travels at speeds near mach 9. This particular variant carries a 250kt nuclear warhead. It's important to be cautious of aircraft weight when carrying the Zircon as it is a heavy and expensive missile.

AGM-84K SLAM-ER (ATA):
The AGM-84K is considered one of the absolute most advanced anti-ship missiles. It carries a large (fill this)kg warhead, flies supersonic, and has an autonomous target acquisition system built into it. It hugs terrain and guides itself along a self path, with or without a target, and can find and destroy its own targets using built-in sensors if one is not selected. It also switches targets when its target is destroyed.

AIM-120D AMRAAM:
A legacy ARH missile, much larger in size than the Scrimtar and Scythe missiles, but also beloved by pilots for its fantastic accuracy and faster speed and acceleration than both missiles. The AIM-120D has an approximate top speed of (fill this) and max range of (fill this too). It's rumored some pilots refuse to take off without an AMRAAM.

AIM-120D AMRAAM (reverse):
Although less conventional, the Boscali defense ministry also created an aft-facing modified AIM-120D. This is primarily recommended for launch at low speeds for targets on the aircraft's rear, but can technically be launched fine by fast-flying aircraft due to the sheer acceleration of the AIM-120D.

AIM-160X SADM:
The SADM is a long-range, kinetic interception and assault missile used in anti-aircraft roles. It is renowned for its cheap price, low weight, tiny size, and high speed profile. It is capable of near mach-3 flight, and can be fired in devastating barrages. Aside from this, it features a unique dual-mode seeker that primarily uses ARH, but switches to an IR fallback if the target is in range and still can be targeted to avoid complete loss of targets.

R-27ER:
An older, medium-to-BVR-range Russian & Chinese missile utilizing the aircraft's radar for SARH interception and assault. It's older but more cost-effective than missiles like the Scrimtar and AMRAAM.
